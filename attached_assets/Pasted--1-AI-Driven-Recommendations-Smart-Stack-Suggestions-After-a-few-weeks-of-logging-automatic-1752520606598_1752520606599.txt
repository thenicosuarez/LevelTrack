
1. AI-Driven Recommendations
Smart Stack Suggestions


After a few weeks of logging, automatically surface “people like you” protocols or top-rated stacks.


Dose Optimization


Flag when you might be under- or over-dosing based on goal (e.g. “you haven’t taken curcumin more than 2 days in a row”).


Predictive Insights


Use your compliance, mood and sleep data to predict which combos will most likely boost energy or recovery.


2. E-commerce & Brand Integration
In-App Supplement Storefront


Link each protocol block to product pages or affiliate SKUs for one-click reordering.


Subscription Management


Let users subscribe to monthly shipments of their active stacks.


Promo & Bundles


Push targeted promos (“Try our vitamin D + K2 bundle”) based on their logged goals.


3. Social / Community Features
Streaks & Badges


Gamify adherence with streak counters, achievement badges (e.g. “14-day compliant”).


Community Boards


Let users share protocols, success stories, before/after journals.


Coach & Friend Sharing


Opt-in “share my calendar” link so a coach or accountability partner can view progress.


4. More Integrations
Apple Health & Google Fit


Pull in sleep, steps, HR data without extra sign-ups.


Calendar Sync


Export scheduled blocks into Google/Outlook so your supplement reminders live alongside your meetings.


Barcode Scanner


Quickly add any supplement by scanning its barcode and auto-populating dosage info.

Supplement Label Scanner
Instead of just barcodes, let users snap a photo of any supplement package.
An AI component (e.g. via OpenAI’s vision/OCR API) parses the label—extracting ingredient names, dosages, serving sizes, nutrition facts, etc.—and auto-populates your protocol grid so you never have to key data into a spreadsheet.


5. Advanced Scheduling & Reminders
Time-of-Day Dosing


Support multiple daily doses (e.g. “take fish oil at 8 AM & 8 PM”).


Custom Push & Email Alerts


Reminders per block, with “snooze” or “mark as taken” right from the notification.


Missed Dose Recovery


If you miss a check-in, auto-suggest a catch-up protocol or skip.


6. Data Portability & Privacy
Export / Backup


One-click CSV/JSON export of all logs, journals, and metrics.


End-to-End Encryption


Give users peace of mind that health data stays private.


GDPR & CCPA Compliance


Data-deletion requests, consent flows, etc.


7. Coach & Enterprise Mode (Future)
Multi-User Dashboards


Enable wellness coaches or small clinics to onboard clients and view aggregate compliance.


Client Notes & Annotations


Coaches can tag concerns (e.g. “ask about gut cleanse side effects”).
